Its system combines autonomous embodied agents, video monitoring agents and sensing systems with an intelligent control layer to detect, understand and respond to any threat, in any environment. **Field intelligence for highly efficient critical asset operations.** Critical assets are becoming a key target in conflicts across Europe and the rest of the world, but operations and security teams and the systems they use are stretched too thin. The Laelaps AI field intelligence system, LEA, can monitor, detect, understand and coordinate responses to any threat, in any environment. A force multiplier, it radically improves the efficiency of operators securing and maintaining critical sites, from military bases, power grids and data centers to factories and hospitals. With Laelaps AI, one human operator can protect and operate multiple sites 24/7 with near total accuracy.

**The Laelaps AI field intelligence stack combines:**

- Embodied agents: AI-powered robots that cover the full site perimeter and send data back to the control center for constant coverage.
- Video monitoring agents: AI that monitors every video feed and flags any threats, day and night in any environment, using existing camera infrastructure.
- Sensing systems: mobile and fixed sensors that can scan and map any terrain, detect and alert human operators to suspicious activity across ground and air domains, flagging any intrusion, from trespassers to drones.
- Control center layer: integrates all data from autonomous agents and sensing systems, transforms it into actionable intelligence for human operators, and ensures all components work together as one team.

**Adaptable and efficient in any field environment.** The field intelligence system is designed to be adaptable and efficient in all environments and conditions, and can integrate with existing operational infrastructure. This fail-safe robustness is the result of combining decision-based robotics models, which mean the autonomous embodied agents cannot hallucinate, with specialised AI models across the stack, including foundational models for night and adverse weather operations, vision-language models, and reinforcement learning for multi-agent coordination and decision-making.

**Built by world-class experts in autonomous systems.** Laelaps AI brings together a team with deep technical expertise across machine learning, physics, engineering and robotics, and venture building experience to revolutionise critical asset operations and resilience. The team is applying their PhD knowledge from world-leading institutions like the University of Cambridge, University of Oxford, Imperial College and University College London, and their industry experience, from Qualcomm to Helsing, to build and enable real-world deployment of autonomous systems.
